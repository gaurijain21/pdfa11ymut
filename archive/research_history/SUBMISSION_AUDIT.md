## Audit conclusion

The project has a credible mutation-testing artifact and a reproducible current formal denominator, but it is not yet submission-ready. The strongest current risks are not the 69/207 arithmetic; they are evidence provenance and interpretation: insufficiently auditable coder identity/independence, M06's bounded machine-checkability, historical M09 bytes that are unavailable for re-audit, and an untagged paper PDF. The current source has now been rebuilt into a fresh, visually inspected PDF; the remaining PDF issue is tagging/venue acceptance rather than source/PDF staleness.

This document is the Phase 1 audit record. Subsequent changes must be listed in `SUBMISSION_FIX_LOG.md` and must not overwrite raw evidence.
