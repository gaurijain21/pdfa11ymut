# Corpus licensing and attribution record

The authoritative collection-level source is the PDF Association's [PDF/UA-1 Reference Suite 1.1](https://pdfa.org/resource/pdfua-reference-suite/). The page identifies a ten-document PDF/UA-1 reference set, describes representative genres and features, and states that the files are freely available under CC BY 4.0. It also identifies the PDF Association and contributing sample-file organizations, including axaio software GmbH, PDFlib GmbH, and Klaas Posselt / einmanncombo.

The governing license text is the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International deed](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The required attribution is appropriate credit, a link to the license, and an indication of changes. This repository therefore records the collection-level license as `CC BY 4.0` and preserves the source reference in `data/corpus_inventory.csv`.

## Provenance resolution

The nine local files are now mapped to suite items by the public item-number and filename convention. The mapping, descriptive attribution, reproducible mirror URLs, and local SHA-256 references are recorded in [`data/corpus_provenance_sources.csv`](data/corpus_provenance_sources.csv) and cross-linked from [`data/corpus_inventory.csv`](data/corpus_inventory.csv). The user has confirmed that the local files were taken from the PDF/UA reference files.

The local set contains items 2-01 through 2-06 and 2-08 through 2-10. It therefore omits the suite's 2-07 item, listed by the public suite index as a Nordic Council of Ministers / TemaNord 2016 publication. The omitted item is not part of this experiment and must not be implied to be present. The current PDF Association page also notes that one suite file was removed pending replacement; this is consistent with documenting the local nine-file subset rather than silently treating it as the complete current download package.

The mapping is provenance evidence, not byte-level proof of the historical download path: the original acquisition archive and download timestamp were not retained. Reproducibility is provided by the recorded source index, public mirror URL, local filename, page count, and SHA-256 hash. No rights beyond the collection's stated CC BY 4.0 terms are inferred.
